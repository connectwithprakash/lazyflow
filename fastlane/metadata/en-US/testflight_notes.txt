What's New in v1.11.0:

• Knowledge Graph (Experimental) — opt in via Settings → AI → Experimental. Builds an on-device graph connecting tasks by people, projects, and topics to improve AI suggestions. To test: enable it, create a few tasks mentioning the same person or project, then check Next Up ordering and tomorrow's Morning Briefing. Reset anytime from Settings → Data & About.
• Redesigned date and time pickers in Add Task — dedicated sheets for date and time block
• Settings reorganized into focused group pages
• iPhone landscape — Focus Mode and onboarding now adapt (rotate during a focus session and tell us what you see)
• Calendar sync hardening — event ownership, undo-safe deletion, ineligible task cleanup

All AI processing is on-device by default. The Knowledge Graph never leaves your phone.
